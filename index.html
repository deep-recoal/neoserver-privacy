Privacy Policy

NeoServer (package in.propterminal.trading), published by PropTerminal · Effective 24 November 2026

NeoServer is a personal trading tool. It connects your phone to your own trading server and to your own broker accounts (Kotak Securities Neo and Fyers). This policy explains what data the app handles and where that data goes.

Summary

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
  • Credentials are stored only on your device and are sent only to the service they belong to.

Data the app handles

DataWhyWhere it goes
Broker login details and session tokens (Kotak Neo, Fyers) To sign in to your broker account, view market data, and place or manage orders that you request Stored on your device. Sent only to Kotak Securities or Fyers, and to your own trading server so it can trade on your behalf
Trading server address and app secret To connect to the trading server you configure Stored on your device. Sent only to that server
Orders, positions, watchlists, strategy settings and P&L To show and manage your trading activity Exchanged between your device, your broker and your own trading server
App PIN / biometric unlock To lock the app The PIN is stored only on your device. Biometric checks are handled by Android, and the app never receives your fingerprint or face data

Third-party services

When you use the app, data is exchanged with your broker (Kotak Securities and/or Fyers) under their own privacy policies. Links to TradingView charts open in your browser, under TradingView's policy. No other third parties receive data from the app.

Security

Connections to brokers use HTTPS. Connections to your trading server use the address you configure, and you should use HTTPS (wss) for it where possible. Locking the app with a PIN or biometrics adds protection on a shared or lost device.

Background operation

During Indian market hours (09:15–15:30 IST, weekdays) the app can run a foreground service with a visible notification. This keeps the connection to your trading server alive while your trades are open. You can stop the service at any time from the notification.

Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Trading records on your own server are kept and deleted under your control. Records held by your broker follow the broker's policy and regulatory requirements.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact the developer at the email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
